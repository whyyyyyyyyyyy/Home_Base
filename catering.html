BBQ Catering

Smoked in-house. Available for pickup or full-service delivery to your event.

Bring the Northwoods BBQ experience to your next event or party. Our smoked in-house BBQ buffets feature mouthwatering ribs, pulled pork, slow-smoked brisket, award-winning chicken, burgers, and brats — the talk of every gathering in the region. Perfect for graduations, weddings, birthday parties, reunions, corporate events, or any celebration, big or small. Available for pickup at the bar or full-service delivery and setup.

Everything is smoked in-house the day of your event — no shortcuts, no reheated food.
