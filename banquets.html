Banquets & Private Events

Graduations, weddings, birthday parties, reunions — nearly 100 years of Northwoods hospitality, right along the Amnicon River.

Rapids Riverside brings nearly a century of Northwoods hospitality to your special event. Our cozy dining room features warm wood tones and rustic charm — beautiful woodwork that's been a local landmark since the Ronchi family era. The covered patio sits right along the Amnicon River, steps from Amnicon Falls State Park, making it a stunning backdrop for any celebration.

Easy to reach from Superior and Duluth (~15–20 min south), with ample parking and a welcoming all-ages atmosphere.
