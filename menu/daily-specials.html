Every Day of the Week

Daily Specials

A different reason to come in every single day. Check back often — specials may rotate seasonally.

Monday

Pizza & Coney Dogs

Start the week right with handmade pizza and classic Coney dogs. Perfect for the whole family.

Tuesday

Mexican Fiesta

Taco Tuesday gets a Northwoods twist. Tacos, queso, and south-of-the-border flavors done right.

Wednesday

Wing Night

Smoked or boneless, tossed in your choice of six signature sauces. The best wings in South Range.

Thursday

Locals Night

A night to celebrate the community. Drink specials and good vibes for the regulars who make this place home.

Friday

Fish Fry

A Wisconsin tradition. Golden-fried fish with all the fixings — the Friday Fish Fry you look forward to all week.

Saturday

Date Night

Saturday nights at the Riverside are something special. Great food, great drinks, and great company.

Sunday

Family Fun Day

Wind down the weekend the right way. Bring the whole family for a relaxed Sunday at the Riverside.
