Our Specialty

The Bourbon Bar

We're serious about bourbon. Our curated selection spans small-batch finds, popular favorites, and rare picks — perfect for the connoisseur or the curious. Ask your bartender for a recommendation.

Whether you prefer it neat, on the rocks, or in one of our handcrafted cocktails, the bourbon bar at Rapids Riverside is a destination in itself.
